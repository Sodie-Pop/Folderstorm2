# Folderstorm2 features for the website

What to highlight on the website and in promotional material, in order of
importance.

## Tier 1

### 1. The multi-monitor workstation

- **The world in a rectangle you choose**: stretch the viewer over several
  monitors and keep the 3D world in one area, set by margins or with
  "Fit to a monitor".
- **Visual setup**: a diagram of your window and monitors. Drag the edges with
  snapping, or click "Put the world on monitor N". Works with monitors of
  different sizes and scaling.
- **Everything follows the world**: camera, HUDs, mouse, dialogs and alerts,
  toolbars along the world's edge, the login screen, and photo mode. Mouselook
  has its own switch.
- **Background around the world**: a colour or your own picture in the
  margins. Fill, fit, stretch, centre or tile it on each monitor, or spread one
  picture across all of them. Brightness control, and each layout can have its
  own background.
- **A desktop on your spare monitors**: shortcuts on a grid, added by drag and
  drop or right-click. Each account has its own desktop.
- **Highlight the types of shortcuts**, especially adding and removing avatar
  attachments:
  - **Clothing and objects**: one click adds them to what you're wearing and
    never replaces your outfit. An "X" on the shortcut of a worn item takes it
    off.
  - **Outfits**: worn after a safety question.
  - **Landmarks**: teleport after a confirmation.
  - **Notecards**: open straight away.
  - **Inventory folders**: open in their own window next to the shortcut.
  - **Any toolbar command**: drag a toolbar button onto the desktop.
  - Shortcuts grey out when the item is gone, in the Trash, or already worn.
- **Make it yours**: rename shortcuts, pick from 40 icons or use your own
  colour or picture, arrange by name, move shortcuts to another monitor, open
  with a single click.
- **Taskbar**: minimised windows become buttons along a margin monitor. It can
  also list every open window. Middle-click closes a window, and a mark shows
  which ones need attention.

### 2. Session host: up to five characters

- **Five characters in one interface**: each runs in its own viewer, and you
  switch between them without logging out.
- **Background modes**: Warm (instant switch), Economy (frees memory) and
  Hibernation (frees the 3D entirely while chat keeps working). Video memory
  is shared fairly, so the character in front gets the most.
- **Docked bar**: one row at the top of every viewer with your characters,
  quick actions, the shared chat and settings.
- **Shared chat**: nearby chat, IMs, groups and conferences for every
  character, separately or all together. Send as any character, start new IMs
  from each character's friends list, and whisper, say or shout.
- **Live monitors**: small live views of your background characters, each in
  its own window with a chat box. Swap with the character in front in one
  click, or use a cycling monitor that shows each character in turn.
- **Attention inbox**: questions from all your characters in one list, each
  with "Switch to answer".
- **Hotkeys**: switch characters from anywhere in Windows (Ctrl+Shift+F1 to
  F5, previous and next).
- **Colour and nickname per character** across the whole interface.
- **Sound and voice follow the character in front.** Background characters can
  play an alert sound for each kind of event (IM, group message, mention,
  offers, teleport offers), with your own sounds and quiet hours.
- **New character profile**: copies your interface setup without any login
  details.
- **Your rules still apply**: RLVa and each character's own settings decide
  what's allowed.

### 3. Layouts and workspaces

- **Layouts**: save the world area, toolbars and window positions, plus the
  viewer window's mode (normal, full screen, or borderless across monitors)
  with an optional size lock.
- **Knows your monitors**: plug in or unplug a monitor, and it offers the
  layout saved for that setup.
- **Workspaces**: which windows are open, including Inventory folder windows
  (up to 16) reopened in their places.
- **Workspace extras**: a graphics profile, a camera view, and HUDs that go on
  when you switch in and come off when you switch out.
- **Switcher**: type to filter, press Enter to apply, favorites first, and a
  preview picture on your current monitors.
- **Quick switching**: a favorites strip under the top bar, a toolbar button
  that flips between two layouts, and keyboard shortcuts.
- **Previous arrangement**: undo any switch, including graphics, camera and
  HUDs.
- **Private by design**: never stores messages or inventory contents.

### 4. Local AI assistant

- **Works with Claude Code, Cursor and Codex**, with one-click setup and a
  connection check.
- **Off by default and on your computer only**: nothing leaves your PC except
  through the viewer.
- **You set the rules**: Read only, Ask before changes, or Custom permissions
  per kind of action, optionally for this session only. Spending L$ and
  teleporting always ask, and it can never delete permanently or empty the
  Trash.
- **Inventory and outfits**: search, organise, rename, move, copy, link, wear
  and take off. Big changes are shown as a plan to approve first.
- **Camera and photos**: camera presets and exact shots, pictures of the
  world, outfit thumbnails.
- **Look around**: location, parcel, nearby avatars, objects, sounds and media.
- **Build and script**: edit notecards and scripts, compile, manage object
  contents, create and edit your own objects, and test scripts (touch, chat
  channels, dialogs).
- **Gestures and posing**: play your gestures and pose your own avatar.
- **Uploads**: local files and pictures, with the price shown before anything
  is spent.
- **Movement**: walk or fly to a point. Teleports always ask.
- **Multi-character**: pick which character the assistant works on.
- **Respects RLVa and creators' permissions.**

## Tier 2

### 5. Gesture boards

- Tiles for your gestures and sounds, filled by dragging items, whole folders
  or selections from your inventory.
- Click a tile to play it.
- Several boards per account, in the order you choose.
- Your own labels, colours, tile sizes and pages.
- Find any tile with Ctrl+F.
- A toolbar button, and RLVa is respected.

### 6. Paste an image into chat

- Paste, drop or choose an image in any chat box.
- It uploads and puts the link in your message. Nothing is sent until you
  press Send.
- Location and camera data are removed from the picture before it uploads.
- Confirmation before each upload.

### 7. Custom Inventory tabs

- Tabs that remember their own filters and search.
- Customize, rename, clone, and pick one of 40 icons.
- **Folder tabs**: limit a tab to chosen folders by dragging folders onto it.
- **Pinned items**: chosen items at the top of a tab.
- **Tab windows**: open any tab as a small window of its own, or show it as a
  flat list.
- **Every Inventory window** can stay on top or switch to a quiet mode that
  shows only the list.
- Works in both inventory styles (Legacy and Aurora).

### 8. Outfit safety and "Restore previous look"

- "Wear" in the right-click menu is off until you turn it on, so you can't put
  something on by mistake.
- Replacing or adding a folder outfit always asks first, with Cancel as the
  default.
- "Restore previous look" brings back what you wore before, in one click.

### 9. Upload a whole folder

- Upload a folder from your computer, and its folder structure is rebuilt in
  Inventory.
- The confirmation lists files by type, new folders, skipped files, name
  warnings and your balance after upload.
- A summary when it's done.

### 10. Bulk rename and move with a preview

- Select 1 to 50 items.
- Rename with a pattern, or move them to a folder found by typing its name.
- See every before and after line, plus any skipped items, before anything
  changes.

### 11. Outfit Gallery thumbnail size

- A slider sets the photo size from 80 to 256 px.

## Tier 3

- **Jump to folder**: type a folder's name or its parent's name and go
  straight there.
- **Account setup backup**:
  - Your whole Folderstorm setup in one file: layouts, desktop, tabs, gesture
    boards and more.
  - Merge or replace each part.
  - A safety backup before every import, with Restore to undo.
  - Optional daily automatic backups.
  - Never includes passwords, logins, chat or e-mail addresses.
- **Up to 16 extra Inventory windows**: each with its own tabs, search and
  filters. Open one with Ctrl+Shift+I or a button, and close them all in one
  click. Layouts place them for you.
- **Smarter Preferences search**: finds "dual monitor", "workspace", "MCP" and
  "undo".
